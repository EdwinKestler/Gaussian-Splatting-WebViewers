Gaussian Splatting Web Viewers

Browser viewers for 3D Gaussian Splatting. The WebGPU viewer is the recommended path: it loads a trained INRIA point_cloud.ply (float covariance + SH0–3) and rasterizes the Kerbl et al. radiance field on the GPU. Compact .splat files are SH0 previews. The original Three.js and A-Frame demos are kept as WebGL fallbacks.
